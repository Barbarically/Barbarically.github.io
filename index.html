Keyline

Keyline is a local Chrome extension that helps its user research keywords. With the user’s permission, it connects directly to Google Ads Keyword Planner to display monthly search volume, daily volume derived as monthly searches divided by 30, CPC, competition, and related keyword ideas.

Keyline does not operate a remote application server. OAuth credentials and settings are stored locally in the user’s Chrome extension storage, and keyword requests are sent directly from the extension to Google’s OAuth and Google Ads API endpoints.
